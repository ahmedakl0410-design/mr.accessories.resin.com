Epoxy Resin Care Guide

Keep Away From Prolonged Direct Sun

Although our resin features premium UV blockers, storing items away from direct sunlight preserves pristine transparency.

Hand Wash with Mild Soap

Clean with warm water, mild dish soap, and a soft microfiber towel. Do not place in dishwasher or use harsh scrubbing pads.

Avoid Extreme Heat Exposure

Our epoxy is heat resistant up to 200°F (93°C). Do not place boiling pots directly onto coasters or trays without a trivet.
